## Deviations and open questions

1. **Section order.** The skeleton H2 texts are used exactly, but "Which Level of Care Fits" (the NextSteps block) is the last body section rather than the third. That follows the brief's rule that every page ends with the level of care, the scope line and the links.
2. **Cocaine has no "When Detox Comes First" section.** The brief allows it ("where applicable") and bars claims of medically dangerous withdrawal. The co-occurring section and FAQ still say detox may come first for alcohol, opioids or benzodiazepines used alongside it.
3. **Trust strip.** The brief says to use it, but it's omitted, following the Home-only decision (see the project notes).
4. **Word counts** exclude the hero, Reviews and CTA: alcohol about 805, cocaine about 805, opioids about 790, benzos about 775. With the hero, all are about 830–860, which is within 800–1,000.
5. **Medicaid in the insurance FAQ answers** mirrors the Home FAQ. Carrier-level confirmation with billing is still open, as it is sitewide.
