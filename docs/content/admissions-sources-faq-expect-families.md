3. **[to confirm] what to bring on day one** (Expect FAQ): the answer lists insurance card, photo ID and a medication list as typical items, plus "we'll tell you exactly". This is standard practice, not sourced.
4. **[to confirm] attendance policy, "Can I take a day off?"** (Expect FAQ): the answer is generic.
