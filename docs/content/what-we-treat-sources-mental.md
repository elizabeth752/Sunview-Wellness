## Deviations and open questions

1. **"Signs the two may be connected" boxes.** They use general, recognizable language, not clinical criteria or statistics. They're my wording and don't come from a source, so the client should review them.
2. **No crisis line (988/911).** The client removed the footer crisis line, so the depression, trauma and bipolar pages answer "What if it gets worse?" with the Q19 referral language only. Confirm whether these clinical pages should carry a crisis resource.
3. **Borderline personality disorder FAQ.** Q16 says "personality disorders" in general; nothing confirms BPD specifically. The answer doesn't claim a BPD specialty. The client should confirm whether BPD can be named.
4. **ADHD FAQ "Do I need an ADHD diagnosis to start?"** The answer says psychiatric evaluation is part of the program. It doesn't claim Sunview diagnoses ADHD. Confirm with the client.
5. **Bipolar page has no "signs" list.** The brief says "no clinical detail beyond the sources", so a short "What matters most here" card replaces it.
