# Meta title and description trim (answer 9.1), 2026-09-24

Rule: final rendered `<title>` ≤ 60 characters, `<meta name="description">` ≤ 155 (counted as rendered text, entities decoded; ™ and ’ count as 1). `Base.astro` renders the `title` prop as-is (no brand suffix appended), so the limit applies to each page's own title string. og:/twitter: title and description use the same props and changed with them. No H1s or visible copy changed.

Scope: 62 indexable pages from `dist/client/**/index.html` (skipped as `noindex`: Humana carrier page, /privacy-policy/, /about-us/, /our-approach/ (old), /thank-you/, 404).

## Result
- Before: 41 pages broke a limit (titles over 60, descriptions over 155, or blog descriptions under 70).
- After: 43 pages changed (the 41 plus Aetna and Cigna, which share the carrier title template). **0 indexable pages** have a title over 60 or under 30, or a description over 155 or under 70. **No duplicate titles or descriptions** across indexable pages.

## Where things were dropped
- **Brand dropped** (keyword and location kept): `/admissions/for-families/` (the original had no brand either), `/programs/psychiatry-medication-management/`. `/blog/insurance-companies-…/`, `/blog/beyond-good-enough-…/` and `/blog/in-vivo-treatment-…/` carry no brand suffix because it didn't fit (the In Vivo title keeps "In Vivo Treatment™"). `/blog/drugs-and-alcohol-…/` and the 28 Days podcast use " | Sunview".
- **Brand shortened to "Sunview"** (the site's existing short form) on several program, condition, admissions and carrier titles.
- **Keyword or location dropped:** none.
- Carrier template is now `{Carrier} Rehab Coverage in West Palm Beach` + the longest brand suffix that fits (`[carrier].astro`), which matches the brief's primary keyword `{carrier} rehab west palm beach`.

## How it's wired
- Blog (`src/pages/blog/[slug].astro`): `post.seoTitle` if set, else article title + " | Sunview Wellness", else + " | Sunview", else the article title alone. `post.metaDescription` overrides `dek` for the meta only. New fields in `src/data/posts.js`: `seoTitle` (3 posts), `metaDescription` (2 posts). H1 still uses `post.title`.
- Team (`src/pages/our-team/[slug].astro`): `metaRole` (title part of `<title>` only) and `metaDescription` in `src/data/team.js`. The visible role and bio are unchanged.
- Everything else: the `metaTitle` / `description` props in each page file.
